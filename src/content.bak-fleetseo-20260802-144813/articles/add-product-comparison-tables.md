Some models offer customizable EQ settings.\n- **Battery Life**: Most earbuds last 4–8 hours per charge, with cases providing additional charges.\n- **Noise Cancellation**: Active noise cancellation (ANC) is great for blocking out ambient noise, while passive isolation relies on fit.\n- **Fit and Comfort**: Secure fit is crucial, especially for workouts.
